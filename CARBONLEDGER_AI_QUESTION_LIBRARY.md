# CarbonLedger OS — AI Question Library & Analytical Intent Reference

This document serves as the master catalog and behavioral contract for the **CarbonLedger Data & Report Copilot**. It specifies the questions users ask when analyzing carbon inventories, audit trails, emissions factors, calculation methodologies, validation flags, and generated compliance reports.

---

## Architecture & Grounding Contract

Every response generated by the AI Copilot must adhere to the following principles:
1. **Grounded in Verified Tenant Data**: Answers must derive from actual uploaded documents, extracted ledger rows, factor lookups, or generated report artifacts belonging to the authenticated workspace.
2. **Zero Hallucination Policy**: If data, emission factors, suppliers, or historical baselines are absent, the AI will explicitly state that the information is unavailable and describe what is required.
3. **Full Audit Traceability**: Any specific metric discussed will reference: `Source File` $\rightarrow$ `Page Number` $\rightarrow$ `Record ID` $\rightarrow$ `Emission Factor ID/Source` $\rightarrow$ `Mathematical Formula` $\rightarrow$ `Final CO2e`.
4. **Deterministic Calculation**: The AI delegates arithmetic to the CarbonLedger Calculation Engine ($Activity \times Unit\ Conversion \times Factor = CO_2e$) rather than performing LLM mental approximations.
